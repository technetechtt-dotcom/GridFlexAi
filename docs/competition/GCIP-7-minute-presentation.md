# GCIP seven-minute presentation script

**Audience:** judges / programme reviewers  
**Company:** Techne Tech · GridFlex AI  
**Mode:** advisory demonstration — physical plant execution remains **locked**  
**Data rule:** frozen synthetic pack `gcip-demo-2026-10-12.1`. “Measured (pack)” ≠ commercial meter settlement.  
**Ask:** **R 1,950,000** for a 90-day telemetry-only pilot.

## Must-say lines

1. “These figures are from a frozen synthetic pack — not a live customer settlement.”
2. “Measured (pack) means methodology practice inside the demo data, not an audited plant meter.”
3. “Recommendations are advisory — physical execution stays locked.”
4. “We ask for R 1.95 million for a 90-day telemetry-only pilot.”

## Must-not-say lines

- Do not name a commercial plant (including Prieska Solar) without written permission.
- Do not imply GridFlex is actuating plant equipment today.
- Do not call the internal reviewer an independent auditor.

Run this script **three times** on the presentation laptop before judges arrive. Once with Wi‑Fi off (`offline-rehearsal.md`).
